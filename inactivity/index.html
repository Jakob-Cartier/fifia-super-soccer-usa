Use this form whenever you cannot attend a scheduled USA team activity or your availability changes.

WHEN TO SEND A NOTICE
  • You cannot attend a scheduled practice.
  • You will miss a match or tournament.
  • You expect to arrive late or leave early.
  • You will be unavailable for several dates.
  • Your availability has changed and staff need to plan the squad.
01Send it as early as possible.
Give staff time to adjust attendance and match plans.
02Use your Roblox username.
That helps staff identify which player sent the notice.
03Update staff if plans change.
Submit another notice if your availability changes again.
Privacy matters. Do not include passwords, verification codes, personal contact details, school information, or private health details. A general reason is sufficient.
