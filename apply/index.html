PLAYER REQUIREMENTS
  • Have a Roblox account and be able to play FIFA Super Soccer.
  • Provide your correct Roblox username so the team can identify you in-game.
  • Choose a primary position. A second position is optional.
  • Show respectful behavior, fair play, and willingness to work with teammates.
  • Be available for agreed practices or matches when your schedule allows, and communicate when you cannot attend.
  • Be open to a short in-game trial or role discussion if the team needs one.

You do not need a perfect record or a specific stat total to apply. The team looks at teamwork, attitude, effort, and roster fit. Squad numbers and match roles are assigned by the team.
