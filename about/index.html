ABOUT THE USA SQUAD.

Learn what the USA National Team represents in Roblox FIFA Super Soccer, how the squad works, and what it means to be part of it.

REPRESENT TOGETHER.

The USA National Team is a custom, community-run squad created for matches and team activities inside Roblox FIFA Super Soccer. The name, crest, and colors give the group a shared identity; the players bring that identity to the pitch through teamwork, effort, and respect.

Being part of the team means more than having your name on a roster. It means showing up when you can, communicating with teammates, supporting the group, and representing the squad positively whether your team wins or loses.

This is a fan-made Roblox team, not an official U.S. Soccer program or real-world national team.

WHAT WE ARE ABOUT

MORE THAN A NAME.

A shared Roblox team identity built around the game and the people playing it.

01

Represent the squad

When you play for the USA team in Roblox FIFA Super Soccer, you represent the group’s crest and colors. Wear the identity with pride and treat teammates and opponents respectfully.

02

Play as a team

Pass, communicate, cover positions, and help one another. Individual skill matters, but teamwork and a positive attitude matter just as much.

03

Keep improving

Practice, learn your role, listen to match feedback, and help the squad grow. You do not need to arrive as a perfect player to apply.

BEING A TEAM MEMBER

WHAT IT MEANS.

Every roster spot comes with a responsibility to the rest of the squad.

  • Respect everyone.Be sportsmanlike with teammates, opponents, and moderators. No harassment, insults, or deliberate disruption.
  • Communicate clearly.Use only team-approved Roblox or team channels for match details. Never share passwords, verification codes, or private contact information.
  • Be reliable.Join agreed practices and matches when available. Let a team moderator know through an approved channel when you cannot attend.
  • Accept your role.Play the position assigned for a match, support substitutes, and understand that lineups can change as the team develops.
  • Keep it fun and fair.Follow Roblox rules and the game’s rules. Winning is great; how we play matters too.
