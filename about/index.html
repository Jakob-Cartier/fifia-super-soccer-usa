The USA National Team is a custom Roblox FIFA Super Soccer squad. The crest and colors represent this team's identity, while the roster brings together wing, midfield, and attacking substitute roles.

Our current squad includes Kayden, Brycen, Tessa, and Ruffles. Brycen wears number 10 and serves as team captain. Ruffles wears number 6 and is the right-wing attacking substitute.
